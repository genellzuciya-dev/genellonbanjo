Banjo Jam BUD

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Banjo Jam BUD is a free iPhone app for beginner banjo players, made by Genell on Banjo and published by Nexus Lab Space LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal information.

The microphone and the tuner

The tuner asks for permission to use your iPhone's microphone so it can hear the pitch of each string. The sound is processed on your phone while the tuner is open. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone; the rest of the app still works.

Stored on your phone

Any settings or choices you make in the app stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Feedback

If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send, including your email address, and use it only to reply and to improve the app. You can ask us to delete your messages at the same address.

Beta testing

If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, device details and feedback you submit in TestFlight with us under Apple's TestFlight terms. We use these only to fix and improve the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We will post any update on this page with a new effective date.
