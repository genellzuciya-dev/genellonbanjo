Banjo Jam BUD

Support

Help and feedback for the Banjo Jam BUD iPhone app

Banjo Jam BUD is a free app for beginner banjo players, with a tuner, chords for common jam tunes and Jam Tune Books. No ads, no account.

Questions, bugs or a tune to suggest

Email info@nexuslabspace.com. For a bug, please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you saw. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Common questions

The tuner isn't hearing my banjo. Check that Banjo Jam BUD has microphone access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, then pick one string at a time close to the phone.

Do I need an account? No. The app has no sign-in and no ads.

Does the tuner record me? No. Sound is processed on your phone while the tuner is open and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

The jam list in Jam Tune Books is included with permission from the Queen Bee Music Association in Santa Fe.
